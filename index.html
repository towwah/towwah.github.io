A NOTE
WHILE IT'S
STILL HAPPENING.

We usually meet people after they know what happened. After the decision worked. After the degree. After the move. After they can explain everything neatly.

TOWWAH starts earlier.

I want to document the moment when someone is still unsure, still choosing, still becoming — and return later to see what changed.

— Jumanah
